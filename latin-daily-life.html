Latin for Daily Life

How to use this quiz

Choose the best answer. After every question, you will see a short explanation. The goal is not only to guess, but to understand the Latin expression.

Meaning: We learn not for school, but for life.

Question 8

Question 9

What does “verba volant, scripta manent” mean?
